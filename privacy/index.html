Privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

In short: ripple. has no accounts and doesn't collect your name, email, location or anything that identifies you. Your meditation history stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We only receive anonymous usage counts to help improve the app.

Your meditation data

Your sessions, minutes, ripples, badges, moods and notes are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for your Apple ID, they also sync through your own private iCloud account, so they follow you to a new iPhone. This data is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We can't see or access it.

Anonymous usage analytics

ripple. uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count how the app is used: for example, that a session started or finished, its length and intention, which sound source was chosen, which badges were earned, and which mood was picked afterwards. These signals contain no personal information, and your notes are never sent. They aren't linked to your identity or used for advertising or tracking.

Apple Music

If you choose to connect Apple Music, ripple. uses Apple's MusicKit to search for and play playlists on your device. Your choices of playlists are saved on your device only.

Notifications

Daily reminders and end-of-session alerts are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No data is sent to a server to deliver them.

Children

ripple. is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete individual sessions in the Calendar tab, turn off iCloud for the app in Settings, or delete the app to remove its data from your device. To remove synced data, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Changes and contact

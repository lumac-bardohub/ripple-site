Common questions

Do I need Apple Music?
No. ripple. has its own built-in soundscapes. Apple Music is optional and needs a subscription.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?
Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on both phones with the same Apple ID. Your sessions sync automatically.

How do I change or turn off the daily reminder?
Tap the small alarm clock at the top right of the Meditate screen.

How does "Silence calls and notifications" work?
iPhone apps can't turn on Do Not Disturb by themselves, so ripple. uses a Focus and a small shortcut that you set up once. Tap "Set it up" on the Meditate screen for step-by-step instructions.

How are ripples earned?
See "How ripples grow" at the bottom of the Progress tab.
